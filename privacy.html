RUDRA AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Welcome to RUDRA AI. RUDRA AI is an artificial intelligence assistant designed to provide conversational assistance, question answering, problem solving and technical guidance.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with RUDRA AI, the service may process information that you voluntarily provide, including messages and other content sent to the assistant.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide responses to user messages.
  • Maintain conversation context.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the service.
  • Process requests made through supported integrations.

3. Instagram Integration

RUDRA AI may integrate with Instagram through Meta's developer APIs. When a user sends a message to an Instagram account connected to RUDRA AI, the message may be processed by RUDRA AI in order to generate and send a response.

RUDRA AI does not request or intentionally collect Instagram passwords.

4. Data Storage

RUDRA AI may temporarily or persistently store conversation information when required to provide conversation context or memory features.

Users should avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, financial information, or other highly sensitive information to the assistant.

5. Third-Party Services

RUDRA AI may use third-party services and APIs to provide functionality, including Meta's Instagram APIs and artificial intelligence model services.

Information processed through third-party services may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of those services.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by RUDRA AI. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interactions with RUDRA AI by contacting the service owner.

8. Children's Privacy

RUDRA AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the RUDRA AI service owner through the contact information associated with the application.

11. Instagram Data

RUDRA AI uses Instagram platform APIs only for the functionality required by the application. Instagram information received through the integration is processed to provide the requested messaging functionality.

RUDRA AI does not sell user information.
